Hi, I'm Coach Matt!

Fitness has changed my life in more ways than I thought possible. I will never forget the first day I walked into a gym, I was shy, nervous and had low self confidence. However I quickly fell in love with the journey, seeing myself change physically was exciting and motivated me. It was the positive mental changes though that really impacted my life. I gained more confidence in myself and my appearance, I felt more capable and motivated to push for more change and continue to grow in all aspects.

My personal journey has instilled a big passion for helping others. Since 2022 I have been fortunate enough to help hundreds of people in their fitness journey improve physically and mentally! If you are looking for change and want to live happier and healthier, do not hesitate to contact me!

Based in New Zealand, I've helped clients transform their health and fitness.
